## Service Level Objectives (SLO)

- set specific target values for SLIs - An SLO is a target value for an SLI. It's the promise you make about service quality - are we delivering to what we want to internally deliver
  ![alt text](image.png)

## Service Level Agreements (SLA)

- contractural agreements with customers
  - The contractural commitment you make with your customers regarding a certain SLI. SLAs should always be less than SLOs

### Why Not Promise More?

Why not promise 99.99% or 99.999% availability?

The answer is **cost** and **opportunity cost**.

Each additional nine of availability requires **exponentially more effort**.

---

### How It Probably Would Look Like:

- **99%** (about 7 hours downtime per month)
- **Relatively Easy**

- **99.9%** (about 43 minutes per month)
- **Requires good practices**

- **99.99%** (about 4 minutes per month)
- **Requires sophisticated automation**

- **99.999%** (about 25 seconds per month)
- **Requires massive investment**
